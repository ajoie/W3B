Events

W3B champions receiving training equipments.

W3B players received new training equipment that will help them practice with greater confidence, consistency, and a stronger sense of belonging on the court. Access to quality equipment is an important part of every athlete’s development, and for many of the girls, receiving these resources was both exciting and motivating. The equipment will support their daily training sessions, allowing them to continue building their basketball skills in a safe and structured environment while feeling prepared and valued as members of the team.

The distribution of the equipment was more than just the delivery of sports gear, it was a meaningful investment in the players’ growth and potential. Each item represented an opportunity for the girls to train more effectively, improve their performance, and pursue their goals with renewed determination. Having the right equipment helps create a positive training experience, encourages regular participation, and gives players the confidence to challenge themselves and reach new levels of achievement.

The moment brought players, coaches, parents, and supporters together around one shared goal: ensuring that girls have access to the tools and opportunities they need to thrive. The atmosphere was filled with excitement, gratitude, and hope as the players received equipment that will accompany them throughout their basketball journey. Coaches spoke about the importance of consistent training, while supporters celebrated the progress the girls have already made and the possibilities that lie ahead.

Beyond its practical value, the equipment also strengthened the players’ sense of teamwork and belonging. Wearing and using the same training resources reinforces the feeling of being part of a community that believes in their success. It sends a powerful message that their efforts matter, their dreams are worth supporting, and they deserve the same opportunities to learn, compete, and grow as any athlete.

This initiative reflects W3B’s commitment to empowering girls through sports and creating an environment where they can develop not only as basketball players but also as confident leaders. With access to proper training equipment, the players are better equipped to strengthen their skills, build resilience, and continue growing both on and off the court. The day served as a reminder that when young girls are given the right support, resources, and encouragement, they gain the confidence to pursue their ambitions and make a lasting impact in their communities.

May 23, 2026
Student Stories

W3B champions on their first game.

Our players stepped onto the court with courage, teamwork, and joy, proudly representing everything they have been working toward throughout their training. The match was an exciting opportunity for the girls to put their skills into practice in a competitive environment and demonstrate how dedication, discipline, and belief in themselves can create real opportunities for growth and success.

For many of the girls, this was more than just a basketball game, it was a memorable milestone. It was the first time they had the chance to experience the excitement and responsibility of representing their team in front of a supportive crowd. Through every pass, rebound, and basket, they gained confidence in their abilities and saw firsthand how the effort they invest during training translates into performance on the court.

The team competed against the girls' basketball team from Agahozo-Shalom Youth Village in a spirited and respectful match. From the opening tip-off, our players displayed strong teamwork, effective communication, and determination. Their hard work paid off as they secured an impressive 100–30 victory. The score reflected not only their basketball skills but also the commitment and consistency they have shown throughout their preparation.

Beyond the result, the game provided valuable lessons in communication, trust, leadership, and resilience. The players encouraged one another through challenges, celebrated each other's successes, and worked together to overcome obstacles during the match. They learned the importance of staying focused, supporting teammates, and maintaining a positive attitude regardless of the circumstances.

Most importantly, the game showcased the tremendous potential of young women when they are given the chance to learn, compete, and lead. The victory was a proud achievement, but the experience itself was even more meaningful. It demonstrated that with training, perseverance, and belief in themselves, these girls can accomplish remarkable things. The match marked another important step in their journey, leaving them motivated to continue developing their skills, pursuing their goals, and inspiring others in their communities.

June 6, 2026
