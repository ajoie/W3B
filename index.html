About Us

Women’s Basketball Beyond Borders (W3B) is a refugee-led initiative based in Mahama Refugee Camp in eastern Rwanda. We empower refugee girls and young women through basketball, education, mentorship, and opportunities to build brighter futures.

Our Programs

We support girls’ growth on the court, in school, and as they plan for their futures.

Basketball Training

Regular coach-led sessions help girls develop basketball skills, discipline, teamwork, confidence, communication, and leadership.

Education Support

We follow up on girls’ education, provide English lessons, identify learning needs, and help arrange extra classes when needed.

Holiday Camps

School-holiday camps bring girls together for basketball, mentorship, life skills, leadership activities, educational sessions, and friendly games in a safe, supportive environment.

Education & Future Pathways

We help girls explore scholarships, boarding-school opportunities, universities, and other pathways that can support their education and ambitions.

Mission

To empower refugee girls from Mahama Refugee Camp through basketball by providing safe spaces, mentorship, leadership development, and opportunities that help them build their future as athletes, leaders, and confident young women.

Our Vision

A future where refugee girls are empowered to lead, compete, and thrive through basketball, transforming their potential into opportunity and their dreams into reality.

Meet the Founder

Elvine Mugishawimana is a refugee from Mahama Refugee Camp and a basketball player. For Elvine, basketball is more than a game; it is a safe space where she found confidence, purpose, friendship, and the freedom to dream beyond her circumstances.

Growing up in a refugee camp, she witnessed many talented girls facing barriers that limited their participation and growth in sport. Despite her passion for basketball, opportunities, support systems, and pathways for development were often limited. These experiences taught her the importance of creating environments where girls feel supported, valued, and empowered.

Inspired by both the challenges she faced and the positive impact basketball had on her life, Elvine founded Women’s Basketball Beyond Borders (W3B). Through W3B, she is committed to ensuring that refugee girls from Mahama Refugee Camp have access to the opportunities she wished she had, opportunities to develop their talents, build confidence, strengthen leadership skills, and create brighter futures through basketball.
