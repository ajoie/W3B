W3B Official Launch in Mahama Camp

W3B officially launched in Mahama Refugee Camp during the holidays with a one-week camp that brought girls together for basketball training, games, mentorship, and community connection.

W3B officially launched in Mahama Refugee Camp during the holidays with a one-week camp that brought girls together for basketball training, games, mentorship, and community connection. The launch was more than a single event; it was a full week of learning, teamwork, confidence-building, and celebration for refugee girls who were stepping into a new basketball journey with W3B.

Throughout the camp, players took part in structured training sessions focused on basketball fundamentals, discipline, communication, and leadership. Coaches guided the girls through drills, fitness work, ball handling, shooting, passing, defense, and game situations, helping both new and experienced players grow in a supportive environment. Each day gave the girls a chance to improve their skills while also learning how to encourage one another, trust their voices, and understand the value of commitment on and off the court.

The week also included games where the players could put their training into practice. These games brought energy and excitement to the court, giving the girls a chance to compete, celebrate progress, and strengthen the bonds they had built during the camp. The matches were not only about winning; they were about learning teamwork, handling pressure, respecting opponents, and discovering confidence through shared effort.

For the Mahama community, the official launch showed what organized sport can mean for girls in a refugee camp. It created a visible and joyful space where girls could be seen as athletes, leaders, and young people with talent and ambition. Families, peers, coaches, and supporters were able to witness the players' growth and celebrate a program built around opportunity, belonging, and empowerment.

The launch week ended with a peace marathon, bringing the community together around unity, resilience, and hope beyond the basketball court. The marathon carried the same spirit as the camp itself: movement, togetherness, and belief in a better future. It was a meaningful way to close the week and mark the beginning of W3B's long-term mission to empower refugee girls through basketball, mentorship, leadership, and community support.
